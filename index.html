Bloomberg at COP31 · Antalya, Türkiye

From Commitment to Capital

Join Bloomberg in Antalya, 10–13 November 2026.

Alongside the UN Climate Change Conference, Bloomberg brings together leaders from finance, industry and government for four days of summits, roundtables and conversation.

  • Kempinski The Dome, Belek

Themes in focus

Four questions for COP31

01 / Finance

Mobilising Capital at Scale

Where will the trillions come from, and what will it take to move private capital into emerging markets at speed?

02 / Energy

Power, Security and the Transition

How are rising power demand and energy security reshaping investment in grids, generation and storage?

03 / Resilience

Resilient Cities and Infrastructure

What does adaptation finance look like in practice, and who pays for resilient cities and supply chains?

04 / Markets

Trade, Carbon and Value Chains

How are carbon markets, trade policy and disclosure rules changing the way companies compete?
