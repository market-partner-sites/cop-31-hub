Turning ambition into action

In June 2026, the COP31 Presidency and Bloomberg Philanthropies announced a new partnership to support the COP31 Action Agenda ahead of the UN Climate Change Conference in Antalya, Türkiye, this November. Unveiled by COP31 President-Designate Murat Kurum and Michael R. Bloomberg, UN Secretary-General’s Special Envoy on Climate Ambition and Solutions, the partnership aims to strengthen collective action, speed up implementation and help close the gap between climate commitments and real-world delivery.

Bloomberg has supported successive COP Presidencies since COP21. At COP31, BloombergNEF will share insights on the low-carbon energy transition and Bloomberg Green will host conversations with leaders, while the Bloomberg Hub in Belek brings policymakers, investors and industry together for four days of summits, roundtables and receptions.

About Bloomberg Philanthropies

Bloomberg Philanthropies works in 700 cities and 150 countries around the world to improve and extend lives. Its work focuses on five areas: the Arts, Education, Environment, Government Innovation and Public Health.

Bloomberg Philanthropies brings together all of Michael R. Bloomberg's giving, including his foundation, corporate and personal philanthropy, as well as Bloomberg Associates, a pro bono consultancy that works in cities around the world. In 2025, Bloomberg Philanthropies distributed $4.3 billion.
